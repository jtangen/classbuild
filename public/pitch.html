ClassBuild  —  open source

Draft a course.
Edit it. Teach it.

A topic, an audience, a chapter count. ClassBuild drafts the syllabus, researches each chapter, writes the slides & quizzes, narrates the lectures, and hands you a course you can edit in the browser.

A course includes
  1. i.Reading chapterhtml · md
  2. ii.Slidespptx
  3. iii.In-class quizdocx
  4. iv.Practice quizhtml
  5. v.Teaching packactivities
  6. vi.Narrated audiomp3
  7. vii.Weekly challengehtml
